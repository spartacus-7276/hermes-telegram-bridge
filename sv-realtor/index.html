How it works

From discovery to an informed conversation

SV Realtor is designed to help you understand commercial property before you speak to anyone about it.

  1. 01

    Discover commercial properties

    Browse curated demonstration listings across Pune's office, retail and logistics micro-markets, and filter by location, type, price and yield.

  2. 02

    Review property information and risks

    Read the fundamentals side by side: tenant profile, lease terms, gross rent, cost assumptions and the specific risks attached to each property.

  3. 03

    Submit an enquiry

    Tell us which property you would like to know more about. In this prototype, enquiries stay in your browser and are not sent anywhere.

  4. 04

    Explore next steps with professionals

    Any real transaction would require independent legal, tax and financial advice, proper due diligence, and appropriately qualified or registered intermediaries.

No transactions happen here. This prototype does not offer, sell, or facilitate fractional ownership, units, securities, or any other investment product, and users cannot buy any interest in a property through it.

Why SV Realtor

Built around clarity, not hype

The concept is simple: show the numbers, show how they were derived, and be explicit about what could go wrong.

Pune-focused property research

Depth before breadth. We start with Pune's commercial corridors — from Hinjewadi and Kharadi to Baner, Wakad and beyond — and the micro-market factors that shape them.

Transparent financial assumptions

Every figure shows its inputs. Gross rent, assumed operating costs and the resulting operating income are itemised, never blended into a single headline number.

Commercial property discovery

Grade A offices, workspaces, high-street retail and logistics in one place, with consistent fields so properties can be compared like for like.

Documented diligence process

A structured checklist approach — title, approvals, lease documents, tenant profile and physical condition — written down so it is clear what has and has not been reviewed.

Clear risk communication

Each listing carries property-specific risks alongside general ones such as illiquidity, vacancy and valuation uncertainty, in plain language.

Investor enquiry support

A simple way to ask questions about a property and be pointed towards the right independent professionals for advice specific to your circumstances.

About SV Realtor

A property intelligence concept, starting with Pune

SV Realtor is a commercial real estate discovery and property intelligence concept. Its aim is to make income-producing commercial property easier to understand — what a building is, who occupies it, how its rent is structured and what risks sit behind the headline yield.

Pune is a natural starting point. Its technology and services corridors have driven sustained demand for office space, while its retail and logistics markets continue to evolve. Each micro-market behaves differently, and we believe that local nuance deserves to be presented clearly.

  • Clarity over hypePlain-language explanations and no promises of returns.
  • Assumptions in the openEvery estimate shows the inputs used to produce it.
  • Local depth firstOne city, understood well, before expanding further.

SV Realtor is

  • A concept for discovering commercial property
  • A way to compare fundamentals and assumptions
  • A starting point for informed questions

SV Realtor is not

  • An offer of securities or investments
  • Registered with SEBI or any other regulator
  • A source of verified valuations or guaranteed income
