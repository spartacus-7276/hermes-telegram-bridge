# Builder pitch video: script (v1)

**Who it's for:** real-estate builders and their sales heads, sent on WhatsApp or shown in a meeting.
**Length:** about 65 seconds of voice (154 words), about 75 seconds with the end card.
**Voice:** English, the same narrator as the last two films (Andrew).
**One idea:** the money is lost between the enquiry and the site visit, and Golden Ratio closes that gap.

---

## The script

| # | Time | Voice-over | On screen | Assets we already have |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-5 s | **How many of last month's enquiries did your team call back within an hour?** | A big question on cream, then a missed-call list filling up | Missed-call screen (reel gr-03 style) |
| 2 | 5-11 s | A buyer finds your project at 10:40 at night. By morning, he's spoken to three other builders. | Karan on the sofa, then "10:40 PM", then three phone icons appearing | `07 AI images` 02 sofa |
| 3 | 11-15 s | Golden Ratio closes the gap between the enquiry and the site visit. | Gold logo, then a thread drawing from "Enquiry" to "Site visit" | Logo kit |
| 4 | 15-26 s | Our WhatsApp AI replies in seconds, day or night. It shares prices and the brochure, answers questions in English or Hinglish, and books the site visit. | The thumb tap on "Instant reply on WhatsApp", then the chat: options card, brochure, "Site visit booked" | 03 thumb tap, `03 WhatsApp AI` chats |
| 5 | 26-38 s | Every lead lands in your own CRM, with the whole conversation. Your manager assigns it in one click. Each salesperson sees only their own leads, and today's follow-ups. | New-lead card, then admin "Move to: Rohan", then Rohan's leads and his follow-ups | `04 CRM screens` 00, 02b, 05, 06 |
| 6 | 38-45 s | Our AI calls every new enquiry back, and calls again on the morning of the visit to confirm. | Phone on the breakfast table ringing, then the live transcript | 07 morning, `05 AI calling` |
| 7 | 45-55 s | And you see everything on one screen: leads, visits and bookings, by channel. Every week, AI tells you where you're losing buyers. | Owner at the wall screen, then company overview, then the AI tips card | 10 owner, `04 CRM screens` 12, `06` tips |
| 8 | 55-60 s | Your name. Your colours. Your projects. | The CRM sign-in page flips through three builder brands and colours | Demo CRM, re-skinned |
| 9 | 60-66 s | Golden Ratio. Book a free demo at goldenratiosolution.com. | End card: logo, website, "Book a free demo" | Logo kit |

**Word count:** 154. At the narrator's pace that's about 63 seconds, which leaves room for the cards to breathe.
