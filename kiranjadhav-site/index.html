Ask a question
What is Modified Technical Analysis?

It is Dr. Kiran Jadhav's reworking of classic technical analysis theories, most of which were written for Western markets in the 1940s. He modified them to suit current Indian market conditions, using derivative data, and they apply to stocks, commodities and currencies. His doctorate is in Modified Technical Analysis Using Derivative Data.

Do I need prior knowledge?

Participants range from common investors to brokers, business channel anchors and actors. Tell us your background on WhatsApp and the team will suggest where to start.

Is there support after the workshop?

Yes. All Technical Analysis participants get lifetime unconditional free support. Participants of the Modified Technical Analysis Workshop can also book a 1-hour session with Dr. Kiran Jadhav free of charge.

Is it online or in-person?

The 1-hour session with Dr. Kiran Jadhav is available online or in person. For the format and dates of the next workshop batch, ask us on WhatsApp.

Do you give stock tips or guarantee returns?

No. Nobody can promise returns. Our research is advisory, markets carry risk and every decision remains yours. The aim of the training is to help you read the market yourself.

How do I access calls?

Subscribers access research calls in the Client Area. Open it between 7:30 am and 9:00 am on market working days, log in and choose your product: Breakout Stocks, Swing Trading, Options, MTLT or Fundamental Calls.

How does the referral programme work?

Give a valid reference and INR 50 is credited to your kiranjadhav.com account immediately. You get another INR 50 when your referral attends the Technical or Fundamental Analysis workshop, and an additional 10% of the subscribed value when they subscribe to any consultancy service.
