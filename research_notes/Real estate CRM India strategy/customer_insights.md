# Customer insights: small/mid Indian builders and brokers/channel partners as CRM buyers

All sources accessed 2026-10-18. Segments: **(a) Builders** = small/mid developers, 1–5 live projects, 3–30 sales/presales staff. **(b) Brokers/CPs** = channel partners, 2–40 agents.

**Method warning for the report writer:**
- WebFetch failed on every domain this session (DNS errors). curl via the proxy also failed (403).
- The shared web-search budget ran out after about 25 searches.
- So every finding below comes from search-result extracts, not from pages I opened. That breaks brief rule 9 ("a search snippet is not a source"). Treat every figure as "snippet-level: re-open the link before quoting it to a client."
- Reddit, Quora, YouTube and Google Play threads did not surface at all. Direct customer-voice quotes are therefore thin.

Tags used: **VERIFIED** (government, regulator, stock-exchange filing, primary job ad), **VENDOR CLAIM** (a company's own marketing/blog/case study, or an industry body's own statement), **USER REPORT** (reviews, practitioner quotes, forum posts). **OLD** = older than 18 months (before about April 2025).

## Q1. How do they manage leads today?

### Takeaway
- **Brokers:** run mainly on WhatsApp chats, notebooks and portal dashboards. Paid CRMs are rare at the small end because of cost, complexity and the need for internet.
- **Builders:** even small Pune builders now write "update leads daily in CRM" into presales job ads. But channel-partner (CP) leads are often still tracked in Excel next to the CRM.
- **No survey found:** I found no survey that measures the share of builders or brokers using Excel, WhatsApp or a CRM.

### Cited Findings
**(b) Brokers**
- "Most real estate agents here track leads in notebooks and WhatsApp chats because existing CRMs are too expensive, too complicated, and require internet they don't always have." This is the founder of a free offline CRM explaining why he built it. **VENDOR CLAIM**, but it describes user behaviour. — [Boring CRM, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/product/boring-crm)
- In India, the UAE and Southeast Asia, "that thread is WhatsApp". A broker "doesn't have one lead source — they have six", including portal enquiries, ads and referrals. **VENDOR CLAIM** — [Chakra HQ](https://chakrahq.com/article/whatsapp-for-real-estate-brokers)
- Over 70% of initial buyer communication in Indian real estate happens on WhatsApp. This is a CRM press release with no primary source given. **VENDOR CLAIM** — [OpenPR "Real Estate CRM"](https://www.openpr.com/news/4581590/real-estate-crm)
- WhatsApp is "already the primary channel for property enquiries in India". **VENDOR CLAIM** — [GreenTick](https://greentick.ai/blogs/whatsapp-api-for-real-estate/)
- Example: a Lucknow broker managed leads from portal dashboards. He spent ₹38,000/month (₹22K on 99acres, ₹16K on MagicBricks) and closed about 1 deal every 9–11 weeks. **VENDOR CLAIM** (vendor case story) — [Codingclave](https://codingclave.com/guides/digital-marketing-for-real-estate-india-2026)
- A vendor pitches "no messy Excel sheets", which signals that Excel is a common incumbent. **VENDOR CLAIM** — [Gallabox webinar](https://gallabox.com/webinars/whatsapp-real-estate)

**(a) Builders**
- Propnivesh (Wakad, Pune) presales job ad: handle inbound and outbound calls, convert leads to prospects, then pass them to sales. "Updating leads daily on the CRM" is required. Salary ₹20,000–30,000/month. The listing has expired. **VERIFIED** (job ad, snippet) — [TeamLease](https://teamlease.com/jobs/presales-executive-jobs-in-pune-propnivesh-private-limited-1759765)
- Estatemint (Baner, Pune) presales job ad: pitch projects, record customer reactions in a CRM, schedule visits with the on-site sales team. Salary ₹10,000–18,000/month. Expired. **VERIFIED** (job ad, snippet) — [TeamLease](https://www.teamlease.com/jobs/presales-executive-jobs-in-pune-estatemint-1125714)
- Tejraj Group (Pune) presales job ad: inbound enquiries from calls, email, website and social media; schedule site visits; follow up regularly. **VERIFIED** (job ad, snippet) — [Saarthi](https://joinsaarthi.com/jobs/tejraj-group/executive-presales/1f37fcc7-fe5e-4017-a5cf-2aefdff0de23)
- Mumbai residential sales job ad: asks for "experience working with CRM tools such as Zoho, HubSpot, Freshsales, or similar". **VERIFIED** (job ad, snippet) — [Weekday](https://jobs.weekday.works/residential-real-estate-sales-specialist-at-blackhole-infiverse-wkdybra7v8)
- "Channel partners source 40–60% of leads for most developers. Teams manage it in Excel alongside the CRM — creating dangerous data inconsistency." **VENDOR CLAIM** — [HomeLead](https://homelead.in/why-builders-lose-real-estate-leads/)

### Inferences
- **Builders:** Excel/Sheets plus WhatsApp is still the default for the smallest builders. The 3–30-staff band increasingly expects "a CRM", because hiring managers screen for it. The incumbent to displace is often a mix: a generic CRM or Sheets for direct leads, plus Excel for CP leads.
- **Brokers:** the real competitor is WhatsApp Business plus the portal dashboard, not another CRM.

### Gaps
- No survey (CREDAI, NAREDCO, Anarock, Knight Frank, portals) gives the % using Excel, WhatsApp or a CRM. A 2025 academic survey of 209 Indian real-estate professionals (89 developers, 120 agents) covers VR and closed-loop marketing only, not CRM use. — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/394548878_Technology_integration_in_India's_real_estate_sector_measuring_awareness_of_virtual_reality_and_closed-loop_marketing)
- No Reddit, Quora or LinkedIn posts surfaced. City-level differences in tooling were not found.

## Q2. Lead source mix, cost per lead (CPL), leads per month, cost per site visit and cost per booking

### Takeaway
- **Meta CPL** for Indian residential is typically about **₹150–₹1,200**. Premium or MMR projects can reach ₹2,000+.
- **Google search CPL** runs higher, about **₹300–₹2,500**, but converts to calls better.
- **Cost per site visit:** sources call ₹3,000–₹8,000 "healthy".
- **Cost per booking:** ranges from about ₹35K (mature campaigns) to more than ₹2 lakh (shared portal leads).
- **Source mix:** CPs are the biggest source in MMR (about 50–60% of transactions). I found no measured mix for small builders elsewhere.
- **Caution:** almost all figures come from agencies or CRM vendors selling the service.

### Cited Findings
**CPL by channel**
- Meta leads for Indian residential: about ₹150–2,000. Google search leads: about ₹300–2,500. **VENDOR CLAIM** (2026 blog) — [Sell.Do](https://www.sell.do/blog/paid-lead-generation-real-estate-india-2026)
- Sell.Do also says click-to-WhatsApp ads usually cost more per lead but less per site visit than native lead forms. **VENDOR CLAIM** — [Sell.Do](https://www.sell.do/blog/paid-lead-generation-real-estate-india-2026)
- OwlClaw 2025 benchmarks for Meta lead forms in Indian real estate (**VENDOR CLAIM**):
  - Typical range ₹500–1,200 in one table, ₹400–900 in another.
  - Average CPL about ₹900. Under ₹500 counts as "good".
  - Lead-to-call rate: 15–25% for Meta vs 25–40% for Google Search.
  - Mid-segment (₹50L–1.5Cr) lead-to-visit about 10%, visit-to-booking about 25%.
  - Sources: [OwlClaw RE benchmarks](https://owlclaw.com/benchmarks/real-estate-marketing-benchmarks), [OwlClaw Meta India](https://owlclaw.com/benchmarks/meta-ads-benchmarks-india)
- Meta CPL by segment: about ₹150–800 for affordable and mid-segment, ₹500–2,000 for premium. **VENDOR CLAIM** — [Aarmus Marketing](https://aarmusmarketing.com/in/blog/real-estate-lead-generation-cost)

**City differences (directional only)**
- Mid-segment CPL in Pune, Bangalore or Noida: ₹150–350. Luxury in Mumbai, Delhi NCR or Gurgaon: over ₹500–1,000. **VENDOR CLAIM** (generic blog) — via search over [LeadScale Media](https://www.leadscalemedia.com/blog/meta-ads-complete-strategy/) / [AS Digital](https://www.asdigitaltechnologies.in/blog/meta-ads-real-estate-leads.html). The extract did not say which of these two said it, so verify.
- Average CPL Mumbai/MMR ₹1,400–2,200 and NCR ₹1,200–2,000. The table sits in the Google section, so it is probably search CPL. No Pune, Bangalore or Hyderabad rows. **VENDOR CLAIM** (April 2026) — [Cognitive Marketing](https://www.cognitivemarketing.in/real-estate-digital-marketing-india/)
- Pune project case: 615+ qualified leads at an average Meta CPL of ₹125.95. **VENDOR CLAIM** (single case) — [FK Digital](https://fkdigital.in/real-estate-meta-ads-case-study/)
- Share of developers advertising under their own name, by city: Hyderabad 28%, Bangalore 26%, Mumbai 20%, Gurgaon 16%, Pune 8%. This measures ad presence, not CPL. **VENDOR CLAIM** (2026) — [Muffin Media](https://muffinmedia.co/blog/state-of-real-estate-advertising-india-2026)

**Portals**
- Portal CPL can look like ₹180–240 while real cost per booking is about ₹2.1 lakh, because leads are shared or not real buyers. The same source gives ₹15K–40K per booking as typical for portal-only strategies. **VENDOR CLAIM** — [Codingclave](https://codingclave.com/guides/digital-marketing-for-real-estate-india-2026)
- Portal list prices, undated (**VENDOR CLAIM**, a CRM vendor quoting portal rates), via [LeadSquared](https://www.leadsquared.com/real-estate-leads-online-india):
  - MagicBricks ₹1,463 for 1 listing for 60 days.
  - 99acres ₹974 for 1 listing for 90 days.
  - "Varies on the basis of city and are negotiable."
- 99acres agent plans (**VENDOR CLAIM**, **OLD**, 2024), via [Privyr](https://www.privyr.com/blog/are-99acres-lead-generation-packages-worth-the-investment/):
  - "Sachet Pack" from ₹1,591 for 2 months.
  - "BOSS" from ₹3,149/month.

**Cost per site visit and cost per booking**
- Cost per site visit of ₹3,000–8,000 is "healthy" for ₹50L–1.5Cr projects. **VENDOR CLAIM** — [Aarmus](https://aarmusmarketing.com/in/blog/real-estate-lead-generation-cost)
- Worked examples, **VENDOR CLAIM**:
  - A ₹120 Meta lead at 1.5% visit conversion costs about ₹8,000 per visit. A ₹1,200 Google lead at 18% costs about ₹6,667 per visit. — [Codingclave](https://codingclave.com/guides/digital-marketing-for-real-estate-india-2026)
  - 100 leads at ₹150 give 12 visits (about ₹1,250 per visit). 300 leads at ₹50 give 6 visits (about ₹2,500 per visit). — [LeadProIO](https://leadproio.com/real-estate-cost-per-lead-india/)
- CP-focused Meta/Google campaigns: about ₹35K per booking "when mature". **VENDOR CLAIM** — [Urban AdMark](https://urbanadmark.com/case-studies/channel-partners)
- Pune RCS-messaging case (**VENDOR CLAIM**) — [Scalify Labs](https://scalifylabs.com/blog/case-study-real-estate-rcs-messaging-pune):
  - About 520 confirmed site visits from a ₹33,300 campaign, about ₹64 per visit.
  - The previous portal-plus-Facebook launch cost ₹1,771 per visit.
  - 68 bookings in 30 days.
  - It relied on an existing database of 75,000 contacts, so it is not typical.

**Source mix**
- Intermediaries handle "nearly 60%" of residential transactions in MMR, per CREDAI-MCHI at its May 2026 expo. Another report says 50–60%. **VENDOR CLAIM** (industry body, via press) — [PropNewsTime](https://propnewstime.com/latestnewsstories/MzAxNTM=/channel-partners-drive-nearly-60-of-mmr-home-sales-says-credai-mchi-at-first-exclusive-property-expo)
- These sharply disagree with a blog that says CPs account for only "around 10–15%" of sales, with no source given. **VENDOR CLAIM** — [BeyondWalls](https://corporate.beyondwalls.com/?p=2375). The CREDAI-MCHI figure is more credible for MMR because it comes from the developers' own body.
- Homesfy (listed brokerage) FY25: direct sales 75% of GTV, MyMagnet CP platform 25%. CP network 14,000+. Pune only about 1% of its business. **VERIFIED** (NSE filing) — [Homesfy investor PPT, Sep 2025](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/HOMESFY_25092025152839_Homesfy_PPT-_BCC.pdf)

**Leads per month**
- "50+ leads per month" claimed for real-estate clients. **VENDOR CLAIM**, no sample size — [IRPR Agency](https://irpr.agency/programmatic-website/real-estate/pune)
- A mid-sized Pune developer using 99acres, MagicBricks and Click-to-WhatsApp gets "hundreds of enquiries a week". **VENDOR CLAIM** — [RichAutomate](https://richautomate.in/use-cases/whatsapp-for-real-estate-builders-in-pune-india-2026)
- Pune district had 16,783 launched units across 177 launch projects in Q2 2024. **OLD**, market-level, not per-builder — via search on [RealtyNMore](https://realtynmore.com/?p=92008)

### Inferences
- **ESTIMATE, leads per month per project for a small builder: about 150–800.**
  - Reasoning: a small Pune builder typically spends about ₹1–3 lakh/month on Meta/Google per live project. This is itself an assumption; I found no spend source.
  - At a blended CPL of about ₹300–900 that gives about 110–1,000 leads.
  - Add portal leads (a few dozen) and CP walk-ins.
  - Low confidence. Validate in discovery calls.
- **ESTIMATE, cost per booking for a small builder on paid digital: about ₹40K–₹1.5 lakh.**
  - Reasoning: CPL ₹600 × lead-to-visit 8–15% × visit-to-booking 5–25% (ranges above).
  - That gives about ₹4,000–7,500 per visit and about ₹16K–₹150K per booking.
  - Cross-check: Urban AdMark's ₹35K mature and Codingclave's ₹15–40K portal-only sit at the low end.
- **Implication for the CRM pitch:** the pitch should be about cost per site visit and cost per booking by source, not CPL. Several sources say CPL alone misleads.
- **Segment differences:**
  - **(b) Brokers:** rely more on portals and on builder-supplied or shared leads, and spend less (₹15K–40K/month portal budgets are plausible from the Lucknow case).
  - **(a) Builders:** the big extra source is CPs, about 50–60% in MMR.

### Gaps
- No independent or portal-published CPL by city. No measured source mix for Pune, Bangalore, Hyderabad or NCR small builders. No current builder-package prices from 99acres, MagicBricks or Housing.com. No hoarding or walk-in share data.

## Q3. Sales process: presales vs closing, site-visit flow, revisits, booking timeline, CP role and commission

### Takeaway
- **(a) Builders split the work:**
  - A **presales calling team** qualifies leads and books site visits. Pay is about ₹10–30K/month, with about 50–80 calls a day.
  - An **on-site closing team** converts visits.
  - Benchmarks: lead-to-visit about 8–15%, visit-to-booking about 5–25%, decision cycle about 90–180 days.
- **(b) CPs bring 50–60% of MMR sales.** The developer pays them about **2–5% of basic sale price (BSP)**, up to 6% plus spot incentives on slow inventory. Payment is often triggered by the buyer paying 10–20% of the agreement value.
- **City-specific commission rates were not found.**

### Cited Findings
**Presales and closing teams**
- Presales role (Pune): pitch, drive visits, record reactions in the CRM, schedule visits with the on-site sales team. **VERIFIED** (job ad) — [Estatemint/TeamLease](https://www.teamlease.com/jobs/presales-executive-jobs-in-pune-estatemint-1125714)
- Presales converts leads to prospects "before passing them to sales". **VERIFIED** (job ad) — [Propnivesh/TeamLease](https://teamlease.com/jobs/presales-executive-jobs-in-pune-propnivesh-private-limited-1759765)
- Pune sales executive ad: 50–80 outbound calls a day and 1–3 customer site visits. **VERIFIED** (job ad, snippet) — [Weekday, Pune Property Circle](https://jobs.weekday.works/real-estate-sales-operations-executive-at-pune-property-circle-wkdyp2o3ef)
- Other ads mention "daily and monthly targets with respect to call volume and sales" (Grium Realty, Pune) and daily/monthly calling targets (Gurgaon). **VERIFIED** (job ads) — [Grium/TeamLease](https://teamlease.com/jobs/presales-executive-jobs-in-pune-grium-realty-1730472), [JobHai Gurgaon](https://www.jobhai.com/hi/sales-business-development-pre-sales-executive-job-in-thakur-job-consultant-sector-39-gurgaon-0-to-4-years-1784283764-7973002-jid)

**Conversion rates and timelines**
- Lead-to-site-visit 8–15%, site-visit-to-booking 5–12% (industry averages, origin not stated). **VENDOR CLAIM** — [Upgrowth](https://upgrowth.in/real-estate-marketing-roi-calculators/)
- Mid-segment lead-to-visit about 10%, visit-to-booking about 25%. **VENDOR CLAIM** — [OwlClaw](https://owlclaw.com/benchmarks/lead-generation-benchmarks-india)
- Decision cycle: "Real estate has a 90–180 day average decision cycle." Buyers who visited but didn't book "need 3–5 follow-up contacts over 2–4 weeks. Most never receive them." **VENDOR CLAIM** — [HomeLead](https://homelead.in/why-builders-lose-real-estate-leads/)
- A buyer selling an existing flat to fund the purchase has a 6–9 month cycle. **VENDOR CLAIM** — [Caller.Digital](https://caller.digital/blog/ai-calling-agent-real-estate-india-2026)
- Typical post-visit objections: price vs a competing project, possession date, floor or view availability, loan eligibility, "family decision pending". Pricing objections go to a sales manager with discretion. **VENDOR CLAIM** — [Caller.Digital](https://caller.digital/blog/ai-calling-agent-real-estate-india-2026)
- Site-visit no-show is about 30% when visits are scheduled without automated reminders. **VENDOR CLAIM**, no source given — [HomeLead](https://homelead.in/why-builders-lose-real-estate-leads/)
- A reminder call the evening before and a confirmation call that morning "moves site visit turn-up materially". **VENDOR CLAIM** — [Caller.Digital](https://caller.digital/blog/ai-calling-agent-real-estate-india-2026)

**CP role and commission**
- Builders typically pay CPs 2–5% of basic sale price, rising to 6% plus spot incentives on slow inventory. "No statute, no RERA rule and no state notification fixes brokerage in India." **VENDOR CLAIM** (Gurgaon/NCR-focused listing site) — [RealtyHunting](https://realtyhunting.com/property-broker-commission-in-india)
- Mumbai: "When it is a purchase from the developer, all brokerage close to 3 to 4 per cent is paid by the developer." Prakkash G Rohiira, Karma Realtors. **USER REPORT** (practitioner quote in press; undated, probably **OLD**) — [DNA India](https://www.dnaindia.com/mumbai/report-slothful-realty-market-is-a-broker-s-boon-2619170)
- Payout terms:
  - Triggers are booking, registration, or a collection threshold, "commonly 10–20% of consideration received".
  - Whether the base is BSP only or all-inclusive can move the fee by 8–12% on a premium unit.
  - The guide covers Pune, Thane and Gurugram but the extract had no rate table.
  - **VENDOR CLAIM** (2026) — [Sell.Do CP payouts](https://www.sell.do/blog/channel-partner-brokerage-payouts-india-2026)
- A sample builder CP form: commission "will be paid only on receiving 20% of Agreement Value". **USER REPORT** (one developer's contract) — [Scribd CP form](https://www.scribd.com/document/730897453/CP-form)
- An escrow-backed commission scheme (SJR Primecorp, Bangalore) is emerging as an answer to payout disputes. **VENDOR CLAIM** (press) — [The Realty Today](https://therealtytoday.com/news/property-guide/escrow-backed-commissions-gain-ground-with-sjr-primecorp-initiative)
- Anarock: "top 5% CPs contribute nearly 40–50% of total bookings". It works with 80,000+ CPs. **VENDOR CLAIM** — [Anarock blog](https://anarock.com/blogs/how-anarock-is-using-generative-ai-predictive-ai-to-transform-channel-partner-sales)
- NAREDCO Maharashtra signed MoUs with 41 CP associations across MMR for HOMETHON 2025, which also covered Pune, Nashik and Nagpur. This shows CPs are organised in associations. **VERIFIED** (press on industry-body action) — [NBMCW](https://www.nbmcw.com/news/naredco-maharashtra-signs-mou-with-41-channel-partner-associations-for-homethon-property-expo-2025.html)

### Inferences
- **CRM must-haves from the process:**
  - Handoff from presales to the site team, with visit scheduling.
  - Visit reminders, because the no-show problem is the most quantified pain.
  - Long nurture sequences over 3–6 months.
  - CP lead registration with a payout-milestone tracker (10–20% collected).
- **For (b) brokers,** the commission statement must handle tiered slabs, BSP vs all-inclusive base, and payout milestones.

### Gaps
- Revisit rate (share of buyers making a second visit, and how they convert) was not found anywhere.
- CP commission by city for Bangalore and Hyderabad was not found. Gurgaon has only a 2–5% listing-site figure.
- Booking-to-agreement timeline was not found.
- Presales-to-closing staff ratio was not found.

## Q7. Features that make them switch from Excel/WhatsApp, and that make them leave a CRM

### Takeaway
- **Reasons to switch (inferred from pains):**
  - Instant automatic capture from all sources (portals, Meta, website, WhatsApp) with auto-assignment.
  - Follow-up and site-visit reminders.
  - WhatsApp inside the CRM.
  - CP lead registration with duplicate protection.
  - Source-wise cost per visit and cost per booking reporting.
- **Reasons to leave:** slowness, rigid reports, poor support or onboarding, per-seat cost creep, and needing constant internet.
- Direct switch/churn evidence is thin.

### Cited Findings
- Generic CRMs "miss essentials like automated follow-ups, custom project workflows, or broker channel tracking". **VENDOR CLAIM** — [Erino](https://erino.io/blog/which-crm-customizes-workflows-for-real-estate-sales-in-india)
- Vendors market these as switch triggers: site-visit reminders, automated follow-ups, auto-logging WhatsApp enquiries. **VENDOR CLAIM** — [Sell.Do](https://www.sell.do/real-estate-crm), [WATI real estate](https://www.wati.io/industries/real-estate/)
- Reasons to leave, from users: not customisable reports, slowness, slow problem resolution. **USER REPORT** — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/selldo/reviews), [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/151826/Sell-Do/reviews/)
- Cost, complexity and internet dependency keep agents on notebooks and WhatsApp. **VENDOR CLAIM** — [Boring CRM](https://www.indiehackers.com/product/boring-crm)
- Per-user pricing "so cost rises with team size" is used as an attack line against Privyr. **VENDOR CLAIM** (competitor) — [RichAutomate](https://richautomate.in/blog/privyr-alternative-india-2026)

### Inferences
- **(a) Builders:** visit outcomes plus reminders, CP registration, and source ROI are the likely switch triggers.
- **(b) Brokers:** portal auto-import, WhatsApp-native workflow and a fast phone experience.
- Flat per-company pricing counters a known objection.

### Gaps
- No churn survey and no "why we left X" posts were retrieved.

## Q9. Inventory, CP portal and post-sales: one tool or separate?

### Takeaway
- **Not found** as buyer-stated preference. Vendors increasingly bundle CP modules and inventory with the CRM. The CP Excel-vs-CRM split is cited as a pain, which suggests builders value CP-in-CRM.
- Post-sales (payment schedules, demand letters) is usually separate (ERP, Tally or Excel). Unverified.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- **(a) Builders with 1–5 projects:** CP registration and simple unit availability inside the CRM probably matter more than post-sales. Post-sales often sits with accounts in Tally/Excel.
- **(b) Brokers** need a simple inventory/listing view and commission tracking, not post-sales.

### Gaps
- No buyer survey. Ask discovery-call question: "Where do your unit availability, CP registrations and demand letters live today?"
