## Q1. Benchmark: 5–8 comparable plans, ₹/month for 5, 15 and 40 users

### Takeaway
Most Indian SMB sales CRMs charge **₹400–₹1,000 per user per month** on annual billing. That works out to roughly ₹2,000–5,000/month for 5 users, ₹6,000–15,000 for 15 users and ₹16,000–40,000 for 40 users. Two vendors already sell **flat per-company plans that undercut the planned tiers**: Kylas Elevate at ₹12,999 flat for unlimited users, and Bitrix24 India at ₹1,910–4,790/month for 5–50 users. The planned Starter (₹3,999 for 5 users, about ₹800 per user) sits at the top of the per-user range for small teams. Growth and Pro are competitive against per-user CRMs but not against Kylas or Bitrix24 on price alone.

### Inferences
- **Pro (₹12,999 for ≤40 users) costs exactly the same as Kylas Elevate (₹12,999, unlimited users)**, and Kylas has a free tier. Bitrix24 Standard (₹4,790 for 50 users) is far cheaper. On the headline number alone, a buyer comparing flat prices will not see Pro as cheap. Pro has to win on real-estate features (site visits, bookings, broker commission, projects with RERA numbers), its own branding, and hands-on setup.
- **Starter (₹3,999 for 5 users, ₹800/user)** costs more than TeleCRM Core (₹2,995), Bigin Express (₹2,000) and Bitrix24 Basic (₹1,910) for 5 users. It is about the same as Makanify Builder (₹5,000) and much cheaper than Sell.Do and LeadSquared Pro. Starter is the tier most exposed to the "cheaper elsewhere" objection.
- **Growth (₹7,999 for 15 users)** is the sweet spot. It is cheaper than TeleCRM (₹8,985–11,985), Makanify Builder (₹15,000), LeadSquared and Sell.Do for 15 users, and about the same as Bigin Premier (₹10,800).
- Real-estate-specific CRMs (Sell.Do, LeadSquared, and Kylas Exceed) cost 3–10x more. The gap between generic cheap CRMs and real-estate-specific premium CRMs is the positioning opening.

## Q2. Is flat per-company pricing an advantage, or do buyers expect per-user?

### Takeaway
Per-user pricing is still the norm among Indian SMB CRMs: TeleCRM, LeadSquared, Bigin, Makanify, Sell.Do and NeoDove all charge per user. But **at least two credible vendors sell flat pricing in India (Kylas, Bitrix24) and use it as a headline weapon**. Flat pricing is therefore a recognised and attractive pitch, not a strange one. It is no longer unique, though, and it only helps if the flat price beats the per-user total at the buyer's team size.

### Cited Findings
- Kylas markets "unlimited users at a flat price" and builds comparison pages around 10-user total cost (₹12,999 vs ₹25,000 for LeadSquared and ₹27,990 for Freshsales) — VENDOR CLAIM [Kylas blog](https://kylas.io/blog/top-10-sales-crm-software-in-india), [Kylas vs Dynamics](https://kylas.io/kylas-vs-dynamics)
- A third-party review frames Kylas' limit as "record volume rather than seats" — 3RD-PARTY [SaaSrat](https://saasrat.com/products/kylas)
- Bitrix24 India prices per organisation in user bands: 5, 50 and 100 users — VERIFIED (search summary) [bitrix24.in/prices](https://www.bitrix24.in/prices/)
- TeleCRM attacks LeadSquared on its per-user price ("Why Pay ₹2,500/User") while pricing per user itself — VENDOR CLAIM [TeleCRM](https://telecrm.in/leadsquared-alternative)
- Per-user is standard elsewhere: TeleCRM ([pricing](https://telecrm.in/pricing)), Bigin ([Bigin](https://www.bigin.com/articles/bigin-india-pricing-clarification.html)), Makanify ([iTechGuides](https://www.itechguides.com/products/makanify/)), LeadSquared ([TechJockey](https://www.techjockey.com/reviews/leadsquared-crm)), Sell.Do ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/151826/Sell-Do/))

### Inferences
- **ESTIMATE:** Flat pricing suits real estate because presales teams churn and grow with each project launch. Owners hate paying per seat for telecallers who leave in 3 months. This is a reasoned guess; no user quotes were found.
- Show the per-user equivalent on the pricing page (Growth = ₹533/user, Pro = ₹325/user) next to TeleCRM's ₹599–799 and Makanify's ₹1,000. That makes the flat-price advantage concrete.
- Band limits (5/15/40) behave like per-user pricing at the band edges. A 6-person team pays 2x Starter. Consider a cheap extra-user add-on so teams can sit between bands.

### Gaps
- No SaaS pricing study and no Indian SMB survey on per-user vs flat preference was found (search budget exhausted).
- No user reviews or Reddit/forum quotes on per-seat pain were collected.

## Q3. How competitors bill WhatsApp and call-minute pass-through

### Takeaway
WhatsApp is almost always a **prepaid wallet**: Meta's per-message rate plus a BSP markup of about **12–20%**, deducted from credit the customer tops up. Interakt recommends keeping at least ₹1,000 in the wallet and offers no pay-later. Real-estate CRMs either pass WhatsApp through "at provider cost" (Makanify) or charge a one-time API setup fee (TeleCRM ₹1,999). **No data was found on how CRMs bill call minutes.**

### Cited Findings
- Interakt: subscription charged to card; WhatsApp conversation charges deducted from a **prepaid wallet**; recommended minimum balance ₹1,000; low-balance alert under ₹1,000; campaigns may pause if the balance goes negative; **no pay-later** — VERIFIED (search summary) [Interakt FAQ](https://www.interakt.shop/faqs/)
- Interakt markup estimated at about 12–15% over Meta (reviewer estimate, not itemised by Interakt). Indian rates quoted: marketing about ₹0.95, utility ₹0.14, authentication ₹0.13 — USER REPORT / competitor blogs [Dupple](https://dupple.com/reviews/interakt), [RichAutomate](https://richautomate.in/compare/interakt)
- AiSensy: prepaid credits required; about 20% markup ($0.01416 vs Meta's $0.0118 per marketing message), per a competitor's comparison. Plans: Free (₹50 credit), Basic ₹1,500/month, Pro ₹3,200/month — VENDOR CLAIM (competitor) [WANotifier](https://wanotifier.com/compare/aisensy-vs-interakt/), [Zoko](https://www.zoko.io/post/aisensy-pricing-plans)

- A wallet removes credit risk for a 2-person team. Never front Meta charges on credit.

## Q4. Cost to serve: VPS, clients per box, email, support time

### Takeaway
Server cost per client is trivial. One Hostinger KVM 2 (8 GB RAM) at about ₹1,199/month on renewal can hold about **80–120 clients at 46 MB each** (ESTIMATE), roughly **₹10–25 per client per month** at 50+ clients, or ₹120 at 10 clients. Email is near-free via Amazon SES (~₹9.65 per 1,000) or ZeptoMail (~₹150 per 10,000). A single Hostinger mailbox caps at **1,000–3,000 emails/day** and should not carry daily digests at scale. **Support time is the real cost**, and no benchmark was found.

### Clients per VPS (ESTIMATE)
- Reasoning: reserve about 1.5–2 GB for the OS, Docker daemon, Caddy and n8n (n8n alone often uses 300–500 MB), then keep 30–40% headroom for spikes, imports and backups.
  - 4 GB box: (4 − 1.5) GB × 0.65 ÷ 46 MB ≈ **35 clients**
  - 8 GB box (Hostinger KVM 2): (8 − 2) × 0.65 ÷ 0.046 ≈ **85 clients** (theoretical ceiling about 130)
  - 16 GB box (KVM 4): (16 − 2) × 0.65 ÷ 0.046 ≈ **195 clients**
- CPU, SQLite write contention during bulk imports, disk I/O for nightly backups, and the blast radius of one server failing will bite before RAM does. A practical cap is about 50 clients per box.
- Server share per client: KVM 2 at ₹1,199 ÷ 10 clients = ₹120; ÷ 50 = **₹24**; ÷ 85 = ₹14.

### Support time
- **Not found.** No source on SMB SaaS support hours per client in India.
- **ESTIMATE:** 3–5 hours in the first month (import, training, Facebook Lead Ads hookup), then 0.5–1.5 hours/month. Reasoning: features are simple, but owners and telecallers in this segment usually need phone or WhatsApp hand-holding. If owner time is valued at ₹500/hour, that is ₹250–750/client/month steady state. Support costs 10–30x more than the server.

### Inferences
- **ESTIMATE, cost to serve:** Starter ≈ ₹300–850/month (server ₹25–120, email ₹5, support ₹250–750) against ₹3,999 revenue, a 79–93% gross margin. Margins rise for Growth and Pro unless support scales with users.
- The ₹15,000 setup fee covers about one hour of manual onboarding (DNS plus copying the n8n flow) plus training, with ample margin.
