Tags used:
- **VERIFIED (extract)**: the vendor's own pricing page or FAQ, read through a search extract and not opened.

- **STALE**: older than 18 months, before April 2025.

Competitor-written comparison blogs (Realatic, Sell.Do, Makanify, Codingclave, Leadrat) are biased, and each is labelled where used.

## Key question 1: What is each product's real price for 5 and 15 users?

### Takeaway
Only 4 of the ~20 products publish usable prices. Makanify, Realatic and Farvision publish per-user prices, and Kylas is reported to have a flat company price. The rest are "contact us" or one-time licences. The cheapest per-user options (Realatic ₹399–1,199, Makanify ₹500–1,000) cost **₹2,000–6,000/month for 5 users** and **₹6,000–18,000 for 15 users**. Kylas is reportedly **flat ₹12,999/month for unlimited users**, the same number as the seller's planned Pro tier. The established builder CRMs (Sell.Do, LeadSquared, Farvision, In4Suite) land at roughly **₹9,000–66,000/month for 5 users**.

### Inferences
- The flat-price market is not empty. Kylas (reported ₹12,999 flat, unlimited users) and Codingclave (₹1,499–5,000/mo, apparently per company) already sell flat. Kylas's figure equals the seller's planned Pro price but with unlimited users, so the seller's "Growth ₹7,999 up to 15" competes mainly against per-user tools. At 15 users, Makanify Builder is ₹15,000, Zoho Standard ₹12,000 and LeadSquared Pro ₹37,500.
- At 5 users, budget per-user tools (Realatic ₹2,000–2,500, Makanify Broker ₹2,500, Zoho Standard ₹4,000) undercut a ₹3,999 flat Starter or sit near it. The flat price wins only from roughly 8 users up against Makanify Builder, or 10 users against Zoho Standard (ESTIMATE).
- The builder-ERP group (Farvision, In4Suite, Tranquil, DaeBuild) costs 5–15x more, or needs ₹80,000–2.4 lakh upfront. It targets mid and large developers, not the seller's segment.
- The Sell.Do figures should be treated as ~₹1,800–3,300/user/mo plus a possible setup fee. The Techjockey ₹1,799 is the only INR figure; it is listed, not confirmed.

### Gaps
- No pricing page was opened directly (see method warning). The Sell.Do, LeadSquared, Kylas and Zoho official pages must be re-read.
- Kylas: which aggregator gives ₹12,999 flat is unclear from the extract (Vivelead/SaaSRat/Neersoft). The official Elevate INR price was not obtained.
- No price found for Propacity, Anarock, Square Yards, BeyondWalls, PropTiger tools, or the portal builder dashboards (99acres/MagicBricks/Housing.com builder packages).
- Salesforce India per-user licence price not obtained in this run.
- Setup fees and go-live time are missing for most products.

## Key question 2: Which features do they ship vs only claim? (A2 feature columns)

### Takeaway
Nearly every vendor claims portal lead capture, WhatsApp and telephony. Independent proof is thin, because almost all evidence is vendor pages or competitor blogs. The clearest documented items:
- Kylas: native 99acres, MagicBricks and Housing integrations through its marketplace, plus Gupshup/Twilio WhatsApp and Ozonetel/Exotel/MyOperator telephony, all documented on its own integration pages.
- Sell.Do: a named inventory module (IRIS: towers, units, holds, cost sheets).
- Anarock: a live channel-partner app (ACP) on both app stores, with named developer partners.
- Inventory, channel-partner portals and post-sales separate builder-grade tools (Sell.Do, Anarock, ERPs) from broker-grade ones (Kylas, Zoho, LeadSquared, Realatic). Competitor blogs say LeadSquared and Zoho "stop at the deal".
