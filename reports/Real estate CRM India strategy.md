# Win ten Pune builders by January

**Bottom line:** Golden Ratio Solution can win 10 paying clients by mid-January 2027, but only if it changes two things first. It must close four gaps that lose demos, and it must stop leading with price. Every close competitor already captures leads from 99acres, MagicBricks and Housing.com, sends reminders and has a phone app; the seller does none of these yet. Each fix is small (under 1 week) and costs about ₹0 in API fees. On price, flat per-company pricing is no longer unique. Kylas sells **₹12,999/month flat for unlimited users**, the same as the planned Pro price, which caps at 40 users. For a 10-person builder team, TeleCRM costs about the same all-in (**≈₹9,800–10,350/month** vs **≈₹9,250** for the seller). So the seller wins on real-estate depth, its own branding, local hands-on support and speed of setup. The recommended changes: cut Starter to **₹2,999**, make Pro unlimited-user, and sell WhatsApp and calling as pass-through add-ons, never inside the flat price. On compliance, DPDP processor duties start **13 May 2027**, possibly brought forward to 13 Nov 2026 if MeitY's proposal is notified. TRAI has banned commercial calls from ordinary 10-digit mobiles since **Feb 2025**. MahaRERA treats WhatsApp messages as adverts that must carry the RERA number and QR code (**₹10,000–50,000 per violation**). The plan: walk The Real Estate Mall Pune Property Expo (31 Oct–1 Nov 2026), work the MahaRERA agent register, sign three ad agencies as resellers, and run paid 30-day pilots.

## 1. Executive summary: ten moves, each with a number

1. **Ship the four demo-blockers in the next 30 days, for ≈₹0 in API fees.**
   - Portal webhooks (99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com), plus a Google Ads lead-form webhook.
   - Visit outcomes (done / no-show / cancelled).
   - A daily reminder email.
   - Each is S effort (under 1 week). Rivals from ₹199/month add-ons ([91Leads](https://91leads.com/real-estate-crm)) up to Kylas and TeleCRM all sell portal intake.
2. **Cut Starter from ₹3,999 to ₹2,999 (5 users), and broker setup from ₹15,000 to ₹4,999.**
   - Today Starter works out to ₹800/user.
   - TeleCRM is ₹599/user ([TeleCRM pricing](https://telecrm.in/pricing), VERIFIED\*) and Makanify's broker plan is ₹500/user ([Makanify](https://makanify.com/crm-for-real-estate-brokers), VERIFIED\*).
3. **Keep Growth at ₹7,999 for 15 users.** It beats every per-user rival at 15 seats:
   - TeleCRM ₹8,985–11,985.
   - Bigin Premier ₹10,800.
   - Makanify Builder ₹15,000.
   - LeadSquared Pro ₹37,500.
4. **Make Pro ₹12,999 with unlimited users.**
   - Kylas Elevate is ₹12,999 flat for unlimited users ([Kylas blog](https://kylas.io/blog/top-10-sales-crm-software-in-india), VENDOR CLAIM).
   - A 40-user cap loses that side-by-side comparison.
   - Extra users cost the seller almost nothing: about 46 MB of memory per client copy, not per user.
5. **Sell WhatsApp as a pass-through add-on: ₹999/month plus Meta's rate +15% from a prepaid wallet.**
   - A typical small builder's WhatsApp bill is about ₹2,130/month at Meta's India rates (₹0.8631 marketing, ₹0.115 utility).
   - The same usage costs ₹4,190–5,250 through AiSensy or Interakt (ESTIMATE).
   - This must never sit inside a ₹2,999–7,999 flat price.
6. **Do not build cloud telephony yet.**
   - Ship tap-to-call from the staff member's own SIM, with a "How did the call go?" prompt (S effort, ₹0).
   - TRAI has barred commercial calls from 10-digit numbers since Feb 2025 ([TRAI regulation](https://trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/Regulation_12022025.pdf), VERIFIED\*), so warn admins.
   - Add Exotel only when a client pays for it (bundles from ₹9,999 prepaid).
7. **Move backups off the server this week.**
   - restic to Backblaze B2 costs ₹0 up to 10 GB and about ₹59/month at 100 GB.
   - Offer AWS S3 Mumbai (about ₹237/month at 100 GB, ESTIMATE) as a paid "data stays in India" option.
8. **Sign a data-processing agreement (DPA) with every client from client #1.**
   - Build consent, do-not-contact, erase and 1-year-log features by **13 May 2027**.
   - Add RERA number and QR footers to all marketing messages now. MahaRERA penalises **₹10,000–50,000 per violation** ([The Realty Today](https://www.therealtytoday.com/news/maharera-makes-qr-code-registration-number-mandatory-in-ads-50000-penalty-for-violations/), SECONDARY).
9. **Run the 90-day funnel: 400 contacts → 60 meetings → 35 demos → 16 paid pilots → 10 paid (ESTIMATE).**
   - Start at [The Real Estate Mall Pune Property Expo](https://www.punepropertiesexpo.in/) on 31 Oct–1 Nov 2026 (60+ developers, free entry).
   - Work the MahaRERA agent register (8,205 agents in the Pune region).
10. **Sign 3 Meta-ads agencies as resellers at 20% recurring for 12 months.**
    - This matches AiSensy and Interakt partner rates ([AiSensy](https://m.aisensy.com/blog/aisensy-partnership-programs/), VENDOR CLAIM).
    - One Growth referral pays the agency ₹1,600/month.

## 4. Customer insights: speed, memory and no-shows cost the most

**Answer:**
- **Main pains.** Builders and brokers lose most money to slow first response, forgotten follow-ups and site-visit no-shows.
- **Builders** split work between a presales calling team and an on-site closing team. They get **50–60% of sales via channel partners in MMR**, and still track CP leads in Excel.
- **Brokers** run on WhatsApp and portal dashboards.
- **Likely tool budgets (ESTIMATE):** brokers **₹1,500–5,000/month**, small builders **₹5,000–20,000/month**.
- **Caution:** almost all "customer voice" found is vendor copy describing customers. Genuine user quotes are few. Confidence is low.
